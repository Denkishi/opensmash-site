Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

OpenSmash-Ultimate is a free, open-source, non-commercial fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Nintendo, Epic Games or Discord. This policy covers the OpenSmash-Ultimate program, its launcher, its online play and this website.

What we collect

  • Playing offline: nothing. Logs (play.log) and crash reports stay on your computer unless you choose to send them to us.
  • Playing online: an anonymous player ID (an Epic Online Services "product user ID" made from your device, no Epic account needed), the player name you choose, your game version and mod set (so you are matched with compatible players), and what you post to the game's own online services while playing: rooms and arenas you open or join, and your fighters' battle records and GSP.
  • Signing in with Discord (optional): your Discord user ID, username and avatar, used to link your OpenSmash player, show your name, and (if you allow it) your Discord status and friends for invites.
  • An OpenSmash account (optional): your email address and a password that the authentication service stores hashed; we never see the password.

We do not collect payment data, contacts, location beyond what an IP address implies, or anything for advertising. We do not sell or rent any data.

Why

To let you play online: find opponents and rooms, connect you to them, keep your GSP and rank, show leaderboards, prevent cheating and abuse, and fix bugs you report.

Who processes it

  • Epic Online Services (Epic Games): player IDs, matchmaking lobbies, peer-to-peer connections and relays, stats and leaderboards. Epic's privacy policy applies to that processing.
  • Discord: sign-in and Rich Presence, only if you link Discord.
  • Our authentication provider: OpenSmash accounts, only if you create one.
  • GitHub: this website and the project's code and issue tracker.

When you play online, other players' games connect to yours, so they can see your IP address, as in any peer-to-peer game.

How long

Online data is kept while your player is active. Battle records and rank are kept to show leaderboards. Ask us and we delete your player, its records and any linked account.

Your choices and rights

You can play offline, never link Discord and never create an account. You can ask for a copy of your data, its correction or its deletion through the project's GitHub issues (or the project's Discord server); for a private request, say so and we will give you a private way to send it. Children under 13 must not use the online features.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date.
